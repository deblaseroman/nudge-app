## 2 — Two nudges around a placement

Add notifications for the placement lifecycle. Two moments, and they're
distinct:

**A — Five minutes before the slot.** The plan says laundry at 10am; at
9:55 the user gets a heads-up. This is the transition warning — the thing
that makes a planned day feel led rather than recorded. Same shape as the
event reminder, shorter lead.

**Cluster them, exactly as event blocks already do.** Placements close
together produce **one** notification before the first, naming the others
— "Laundry at 10, then reading at 11." Reuse the event clustering
mechanism rather than writing a second one; say whether the existing
window constant suits placements or needs its own.

Without this, a four-task timeline produces four warnings and exhausts the
budget before anything else fires.

**B — After the slot passes unstarted.** The time came and went and the
task is still open and untouched. This is the case that produced silence
all day.

Neither exists. `getAhead` fires on an approaching deadline, `dueSoon` on
an imminent one, `idle` asks once at wake+3h whether anything's been
started. None of them notice the plan's own schedule.

**Whether these are one kind or two is your call** — they share a target
and differ only in timing, but they'd want separate outcome data if you
ever care which one lands better. Say which you chose and why.

**Persistence for B.** This should be the most persistent kind in the app;
the user has explicitly said they'd rather be nudged than left alone.
Propose a schedule — once at the slot then hourly until done, or a fixed
number of attempts. A placement missed by ten minutes is different from
one missed by four hours.

**Copy.** `DESIGN.md` — factual, no verdict. "Laundry was set for 10am" is
a fact. "You still haven't done laundry" is a reproach.

Both discretionary: count against budget, respect quiet hours and busy
windows. B should be the kind most willing to spend budget, not one that
ignores the gates.

## 3 — Raise the daily budget to 10

`dailyNudgeBudget` is 3, which is why persistent follow-ups would crowd
out everything else. Raise it to **10** — deliberately high. This isn't a
final value; it's so I can see everything the arbiter would fire and
decide from real days which kinds earn their place.

Say what else that changes downstream. Spacing may now be the binding
constraint rather than the budget, and if so that's worth knowing — a
budget of 10 with 90-minute spacing across a waking day caps out well
below 10 anyway. Report the effective ceiling.
