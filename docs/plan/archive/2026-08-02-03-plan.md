## 1 — A prep task only places on its own due date

Observed on device: two study tasks for the same exam, due on **different
days**, both placed today. `ExamPrepSweep` correctly creates one per day;
the planner then treats them as ordinary dated candidates and picks by
score, so tomorrow's block competes for today's gaps. That defeats the
entire point of spreading prep across days.

Fix: a task with `source == "prep"` is a placement candidate **only on the
day it's due**.

Note that `planMaxPrepPlacementsPerRun = 2` was masking this — the cap
stopped a third, which is why two appeared rather than four. Once the
day-scoping lands, reconsider whether the cap is still needed; say what
you think and why, but don't remove it without saying so.

Check whether the same leak applies to any other dated task. If the
planner generally pulls future-dated tasks into today's gaps, that's a
broader problem than prep and worth reporting even if it's out of scope
here.

## 2 — Tasks get appropriate hours

Observed on device: the planner placed **"Call Grandma" at 10:30 PM.** It
knows where the gaps are and nothing about when a task is *appropriate*.
Same failure would put a grocery run at 11 PM or a call to an office on a
Sunday evening.

**Add a `timeWindow` field to the classification** — the same call that
already assigns stakes and `prepLeadDays`. No new API call.

Bands, coarse and few:

- **`anytime`** — no constraint. Study, reading, laundry, tidying, writing.
  **This is the default** and should be the common case; missing or
  unrecognised values fall here.
- **`daytime`** — reasonable waking hours. Calling people, errands, chores
  that involve leaving the house or making noise.
- **`businessHours`** — weekday working hours. Calling an office, a bank, a
  doctor's front desk, anything with staff.

Boundaries in `NudgeConfig` — not hardcoded, and not precise-looking. This
is a judgment about a task's nature, so coarse bands only; do not invent
per-task hour ranges.

**The planner respects it:** when placing, skip gaps outside the task's
band. **If no gap in-band exists, leave the task unplaced** — better
unplaced than at a time that makes it undoable. Report that case in the
trace.

**Deterministic fallback** for calendar imports and any task without a
classification, following `inferStakes`' pattern. Keep it conservative: a
wrong `anytime` is invisible, a wrong `businessHours` blocks placement.

**This is the prerequisite for errand pairing** (`ROADMAP.md` §3) — that
feature needs the AI to reason about what a task *is*, and this is the
first field of that kind. Say in the report whether `timeWindow` is the
right place to also carry away-from-home, or whether that wants its own
field.
