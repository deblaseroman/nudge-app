**Status:** APPROVED — Roman, Sep 13 2026, in chat ("please follow through

**Cycle ID:** 2026-09-13-01
**Source:** Roman's airport use case — "pick up a friend from the airport
5 days from now… tasks and events with time and days attached need to have
a way to be glued to the specific day and time." Traced in chat: events and
PLACED tasks were already glued; the bleed was day-only future intents
reaching the "what should I do now" paths.

## The invariant

> An item that carries a day belongs to that day on every surface. A task
> intended for a FUTURE day is invisible to every now-path — planner
> candidates, morning naming, idle targeting, session suggestions — until
> its day arrives. Deadline tasks are deliberately NOT glued (working ahead
> of a due date is the point of planning); a PAST intent day unglues (a
> slipped intention is fair game again).

## Items

1. `NudgeTask.intentIsFuture(asOf:)` — the one rule, on the model (widget-
   safe), documented as the day-integrity invariant.
2. `DayPlanEngine` — plan AND scored candidate pools exclude future
   intents (a stated day outranks a stated order).
3. `DayPlanRefiner` — same two-pool filter.
4. `NudgeArbiter.buildIdleCandidates` — attach-target excludes future
   intents, keyed on the FIRE date.
5. `NudgeArbiter.morningPromptRanking` — pool excludes future intents,
   keyed on the fire date.
6. `NudgeNotificationService.pickTopOpenTask` + `TasksTabView.
   suggestedSessionTask` — tap-time suggestions exclude future intents.
7. `CONTEXT.md` census — idle + morning rows and the stamp, same commit.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Display glue (Today/Tomorrow/This-week lenses) — separate drafted plan.
- The Nudge calendar (read-only month glance) — future cycle, design
  settled in chat: tap-to-edit via the existing sheet, no drag, pure
  display, nothing new for the AI to read.
- Intent-aware editor (Reschedule must move `intendedDate`, not mint a
  deadline) — required before/with the calendar cycle.
- `NudgeCopyService` ranking mirrors — unfiltered, harmless: cached copy
  only ever swaps onto candidates the arbiter actually scheduled.
