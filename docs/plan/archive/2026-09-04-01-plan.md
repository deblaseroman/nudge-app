## Why this exists

The Today tab's plan section admits by `sequenceIndex` — plan membership,
not date. That was survivable when every plan was for today; cycle
2026-09-03-01 made multi-day intentions first-class ("study python tomorrow
at 9am, then…"), so a tomorrow plan now sits under Today on the day it
ISN'T for. The tab partition diagnosis (2026-09-02) called this out and
deferred it pending a product decision about what a plan's day is; the
deadline/intent cycle made the decision buildable: **a task's day is its
`intendedDate`, else the day it's placed.** Deadline-only tasks are NOT
"scheduled" — they keep living in Unscheduled/Overdue exactly as now
(Roman's word was "already scheduled"; a bare due date is owed, not
scheduled).

## Item 1 — Day membership helpers

In `TasksTabView` (presentation only, no model change):

- `scheduledDay(of task) -> Date?` = `intendedDate` ?? startOfDay of
  `plannedStartDate` ?? nil. Events keep their existing anchor
  (`specificTime ?? dueDate`).
- A PLAN task (`sequenceIndex != nil`) with no day of its own reads as
  today — a dateless ordered plan is today's plan, exactly the current
  semantics.

## Item 2 — Lenses under Today, mirroring the Events pattern

`TodayLens: Today | Tomorrow | This week` — same chip strip as `EventLens`,
same visual treatment, not persisted across launches (like `eventLens`).

- **Today**: the reorderable numbered plan section (plan tasks whose day is
  today), then today's EVENTS (time-ordered, `EventRowView`), then today's
  scheduled tasks (placed order, slot-tinted) — the existing rows, plus
  events, minus other days' plan tasks. Empty state: the Plan-my-day
  button, as now.
- **Tomorrow**: tomorrow's plan tasks (numbered, NOT drag-reorderable — a
  sequence spanning days can't be coherently reordered from one day's
  list), tomorrow's events, tomorrow's scheduled tasks. No slot tints
  (slots describe today's timeline).
- **This week**: days +2 through +7, grouped by day with the Events tab's
  day-section idiom ("Friday · Sep 6"), each day holding its events and
  scheduled tasks. Empty state: quiet one-liner.

The tab strip's `tabHasContent(.today)` widens to any lens having content,
so the content-indicator stroke can't promise an empty tab.

## Item 3 — Nothing else moves

- `Unscheduled` unchanged (still the not-scheduled-today residual —
  narrowing it is a later decision once this ships and Roman sees the
  overlap in practice).
- `suggestedSessionTask`, `hasActivePlan`, the timeline, the widget, and
  every arbiter input: untouched. **Work-order item 5 does not apply** —
  this cycle is pure list presentation; say so in the report rather than
  skipping silently.
- Slot tints stay today-only by design.

## Evidence it worked

  (or today's real items).
- An event tonight appears in Today→Today above the task rows.
- Events tab unchanged; Unscheduled unchanged; drag-reorder still works in
  the Today lens.
