Overview notification-flow-overview.mmd

Labeled arrows are the main path: a trigger runs the arbiter, the arbiter picks the body and adds the request, iOS delivers it to the delegate. The Screen Time extension adds on its own path. Unlabeled arrows are explained in the close-ups. Click a box to jump to its close-up.

Arbiter run notification-flow-arbiter.mmd

One call to reevaluate, from debounce to the request. Gates are drawn in the order they are checked. Builder inputs are in the next section; gate inputs are in the table under the figure.

reads: UserProfile (notificationsEnabled, wake, bedtime, quiet hours), NudgeConfig (budget 10, spacing 90, cooldown 90, quiet buffers 30/60, event buffers 15/90), SessionCoordinator (isSessionActive, sessionEnd), App Group (scheduledIDs, five history maps, lastFocusSessionStartedAt), NudgeTask (placed tasks for the slot gate, events for the busy gate), NudgeOutcome (future-pending and stale rows in cancelAll). After scheduling, reevaluate calls DistractionMonitor.writeSnapshot.

Delivery and taps

What happens after iOS delivers a request. First the delegate's branches, then the foreground sweep that records outcomes and the Settings test button.

reads: UNNotificationContent.userInfo (kind, taskID, goalID) and categoryIdentifier; NudgeTask (top open task for Not yet, the task for Start session); UserProfile.name for the session; AppOpenLog, nudge.lastFocusSessionStartedAt, CompletedTaskRecord and NudgeTask.createdAt in the classifier; NudgeConfig outcomeClassificationGraceMinutes 90, outcomeActionWindowMinutes 60.

Screen Time extension notification-flow-screentime.mmd

The app arms DeviceActivity schedules and writes a snapshot; the extension process decides and posts, reading only App Group defaults.

reads: UserProfile wake and bedtime (schedule window and snapshot); DistractionSettings selectionData, dailyLimitMinutes 30, limitLadderEnabled, mirrorEnabled; NudgeTask day lens and today's events, CompletedTaskRecord latest, nudge.lastFocusSessionStartedAt (snapshot); entitlements family-controls and app group on Nudge and NudgeActivityMonitor; NudgeActivityMonitor/Info.plist extension point. The registered-signature key nudge.distractions.registeredSignature stops a launch from restarting the schedules.

AI copy notification-flow-copy.mmd

The text of an arbiter nudge: generated ahead of time, cached, then validated and filled when the request is built. No API call sits in the arbiter's path.

reads: NudgeConfig copyCacheDays 3, copyGenTasksPerKind 4, copyHistoryLimit 40, copyGenMinMinutesBetween 15, copyGenMaxPerDay 6; NudgeTask isComplete, isInformationalEvent, dueDate, specificTime, sortDeadline, plannedStartDate, stakes; NudgeGoal.title; UserProfile.name; Secrets.plist ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.

Launch, triggers, widget, permission

Four small stories: how the delegate, categories and background task are wired at launch; every caller of reevaluate; what the widget does to pending requests; and where notification permission is asked and shown.

reads: UserProfile (the notificationToken fields: bedtime, wake, notificationsEnabled, nine per-kind toggles, quiet-hours toggle and times); Nudge/Info.plist; the authorization status from UNUserNotificationCenter; nudge.arb.widgetMutated and nudge.arb.scheduledIDs. Targets searched

From Nudge.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj: four native targets. Three touch notifications. NudgeActivityReport (ExtensionKit report renderer) contains no notification code.

  • Nudge (app): arbiter, delegate, test button, categories, authorization, AI copy, Screen Time arming. Entitlements: app group, family-controls. Info.plist: BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers, UIBackgroundModes fetch.
  • NudgeWidgetExtension: removes pending arbiter requests for a task completed from the widget and raises the widgetMutated flag. Adds nothing. Entitlements: app group.
  • NudgeActivityMonitor: posts the limit ladder and mirror notifications with placeholder copy. Entitlements: family-controls, app group. Info.plist: deviceactivity.monitor-extension.
  • NudgeActivityReport: no notification requests.

Exists but has no effect

Only the fatigue gate is drawn (dashed, in the arbiter close-up). Everything here is in the complete reference diagram.

  1. NudgeConfig.fatigueGateEnabled = false switches off the per-task fatigue gate in passesGates, so taskFatigueCount never runs and the classifier's .ignored rows and the delegate's .dismissed rows are read by no live decision.
  2. recordFeedback → NudgeOutcome.feedback / feedbackAt: written on the 👎 action, read only by the DEBUG dump in NudgeOutcomeClassifier.
  3. NudgeOutcome.firedAt is never written.
  4. NudgeCandidate.receptivity is not part of scoring; NudgeCandidate.interruption is overridden by tier.interruption in schedule(); quadrant is printed in DEBUG only.
  5. NudgeNotificationService.configure() has an empty body; scheduleAllNotifications() is empty and nothing calls it; pickTopOpenTask() is called by nothing.
  6. Notification.Name.nudgeIdleNotYetTapped is observed by TasksTabView but never posted; pendingIdleTaskIDKey / pendingIdleTaskDateKey are read and cleared by TasksTabView.consumePendingIdleTask but never written.
  7. .providesAppNotificationSettings is requested, but no userNotificationCenter(_:openSettingsFor:) delegate method exists, so the in-app settings link leads nowhere specific.
  8. UserProfile legacy toggles (eveningCheckIn, deadlinePrep, windDown, habitReminder, monthlyCheckIn, smartNotifications, streak, reEngagement, milestone) and the integer quietHoursStart / quietHoursEnd are stored and never read.
  9. SentNotificationFlag and NotificationEvent (with SmartNotificationType and NotificationTone) are in all three schema lists and never inserted or fetched.
  10. category.getAhead is registered but no request has carried it since the Aug 2026 split.
  11. NudgeArbiter debug*Impact, debugDumpPendingCensus, hadCalendarEvent, legacyInsideAwakeWindow are DEBUG-only and schedule nothing; appGroupID and idleDismissAppGroupID are declared and never read.
  12. EvalHarness and CaptureHistoryExporter call reevaluate only under DEBUG launch arguments.

Not dead but worth knowing: goalLapseNotificationsEnabled is read by its builder yet is missing from ContentView's notificationToken, so flipping it waits for the next unrelated reevaluate.
