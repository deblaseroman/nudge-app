How Nudge Tells Time

The app runs on three overlapping definitions of "when" — the calendar day, the user's awake window, and the today-horizon. Most of the behavior you've noticed at the edges lives in the seams between them.

Roman DeBlase · Nudge

1.0One day, four windows

A single calendar day (example: wake 08:00, bed 23:00) is carved into nested windows, each owned by a different subsystem. An item can be perfectly valid in one window and invisible in another — that's not one bug, it's the architecture, and the seams are where your observations live.

2.0The horizon — detail collapses one day out

The app's model of today is rich: a drawn timeline, colored slots, a plan, placement nudges. Its model of tomorrow is a handful of date fields with almost no surface to live on. Data survives past today; rendering mostly doesn't — so future items historically leaked into today's surfaces or vanished, because nothing else claimed them.

3.0Day rollover — "tomorrow becomes today" happens at first open, not midnight

Nothing runs at 00:00. The store still holds yesterday's shape until the app next wakes — a background refresh around 6 am, or your first foreground of the day, whichever comes first. Only then does the sweep chain reshape the world for the new day.

4.0Known gaps at the edges — your observations, located

Each entry names the seam it lives in, so a fix targets a mechanism instead of a symptom.

  • Events outside wake−30 → bed now draw at their real time. FIXED — Sep 13
    Seam: FIG 1, band 2. The strip's render window stretches to cover every one of today's events (an event's time is a fact); the hour-before notification always fired and is unchanged. TASK placement stays clamped inside wake−30 → bed — taps beyond it propose the nearest in-window time. Tasks outside the day's bounds are a noted, deliberately deferred question ("sometimes people need to do things outside their planning window").
  • Future days have a surface: the Today / Tomorrow / This-week lenses. BUILT — Sep 13 (cycle 2026-09-04-01)
    Seam: FIG 2, now green. Day membership = intent day ?? placement day; each lens shows its day's plan rows, events, and tasks. Deadline-only tasks deliberately stay in Unscheduled — owed is not scheduled. Beyond +7 remains data-only until the Nudge calendar (step 3).
  • Tomorrow's plan tasks no longer show under Today. FIXED — same cycle
    Plan membership now reads the task's day: today's slice stays reorderable; a future day's plan rows render under that day, numbered but static.
  • Future-day items can no longer bleed into today's now-paths. FIXED — Sep 13 (day-integrity glue)
    The airport case: a task intended days out could be seated into today by Plan-my-day, named by the morning prompt, or proposed by the idle nudge. One invariant now guards all six now-paths: a future intent is invisible until its day arrives. Events and placed tasks were already glued; deadlines deliberately stay workable early.
  • Planning beyond today. BUILT — Sep 13 (cycle 2026-09-13-03)
    The engine is day-parameterized (zero AI, zero tokens). Two doors: "Plan tomorrow" on the Tomorrow lens, "Plan this day" on a future calendar day. Staleness rule per Roman: future-day placements are treated as manual — that day's morning auto-run respects them and refills the remaining gaps, never silently deleting advance work.
  • The stale zone between midnight and first open. ACCEPTED — by design (Roman, Sep 13)
    Seam: FIG 3. The sweeps run before anything renders on first open, so the stale window is invisible to the user; the ~6 am background task narrows it. Midnight-timer mitigation judged complexity spent on sleeping hours. Documented shrug, on purpose.
  • An intention can no longer fabricate a deadline. FIXED — cycle 2026-09-03-01
    The old worst edge case: every stated day became "due at 23:59," making countdowns, false overdues, and 6 am dueSoon fires. Kept here because the 23:59 convention (FIG 1) still applies to real deadlines — that part is correct behavior.
  • Past-midnight bedtimes. FIXED — DayWindow
    A bedtime at/after 00:00 counts as the next calendar day. Historically this emptied the planning window and quiet hours silently; the shared derivation fixed both. The strip guards separately with a 16-hour fallback.
